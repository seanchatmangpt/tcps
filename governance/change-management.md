# Change management

Every change must identify its exact base, authority impact, actuator impact, schema impact, generated impact, verification commands, and release standing.

Changes that expand DO authority require all of the following before implementation promotion:

1. explicit operation schema;
2. policy vocabulary change;
3. threat model update;
4. postcondition verifier;
5. refusal semantics;
6. rollback or irreversibility classification;
7. receipt representation;
8. unit and integration witnesses;
9. replay semantics;
10. release-control review.

Generated outputs are changed through ontology/template inputs and projection, not as independent design surfaces.
